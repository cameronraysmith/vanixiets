The change lands after compose-hosts-from-cohesive-aspects as thirteen delivery steps (design D10), each one commit in one pull request, rebased onto the previous step's merge and accepted on one full `just drv-diff <parent> <step commit> openspec/changes/seam-secrets-away-from-clan-vars/drv-delta/<step>.txt` (about 16 minutes). The implementer of a step does not run that diff: the step's last task returns the exact command, and the operator or main session runs it, re-judges with `just drv-diff-rejudge` after any allow-list edit, and records the evidence.
Groups 2 to 14 are the steps in order; within them, steps 2 to 12 (groups 3 to 13) may be reordered, except that step 10 (group 11) follows step 9 (group 10) (design D10), and each group is self-contained.
Verification is by evaluation (`nix eval`), by building a named check (`nix build`), or by the drvPath diff; reading the source is never the verification for a Nix output. Every new or edited file passes `treefmt` before its step's commit is published. A run expected to exceed 5 minutes is announced first with its duration, what it proves that a cheaper run cannot, and what waits on it.
**[operator]** marks a task that waits on the operator.
