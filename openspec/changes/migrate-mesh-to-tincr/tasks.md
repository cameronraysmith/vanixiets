Groups follow the task-group DAG in `design.md` ("Task-group DAG"): group 1 is the contract (C) and lands first; groups 2–8 (R, T, A, K, M, B, S) are authored in parallel from it by subagents owning disjoint files (`plan.md` "File ownership"), each as one stacked pull request on top of group 1; group 9 (V) deploys and accepts the mirror; group 10 (X) is the operator's cutover decision; groups 11 (F) and 12 (Z) run only after an approval in group 10; group 13 (E) closes out.
Items marked **[operator]** need the operator present and confirming in the session, with the confirmation and its time recorded; items marked **[gate]** stop the work when their condition fails, and the failure goes to the operator before anything later runs.
Nix-managed outputs are verified by evaluating (`nix eval`), building (`nix build`) or reading the delivered path on the host after activation, never by reading source; each item names which.
Every clan vars command carries `CLAN_NO_COMMIT=1` and names its generators, runs in the development join's working copy, and its files are routed into the owning change with `git rev-parse HEAD` unchanged (`plan.md` "Global constraints").
Every deploy or activation item ends by reading every post since its start from `journalctl -u fleet-alert -u 'observability-notify@*' --since <start>` on magnetite and `/var/lib/otel-envelope/notify.log` on stibnite, recording each with its cause; a post no record explains is a finding.
Every item that adds or changes a check records its evaluation cost before and after (`NIX_SHOW_STATS=1 NIX_SHOW_STATS_PATH=<file> nix eval --option eval-cache false --raw .#checks.x86_64-linux.<check>.drvPath`, `.gc.totalBytes` and `.cpuTime`), and a figure over 536,870,912 B stops the item.
Evidence logs go to `logs/cam109-evidence/<item>-<slug>-<UTC timestamp>.log`; `logs/` is git-ignored.
Pull-request bodies say `Part of CAM-109.` and never a closing keyword.
